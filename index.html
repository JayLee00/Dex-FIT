DexFIT

Force Informed Transfer for dexterous manipulation using a kinematically isomorphic tactile glove. A 16-DoF wearable glove that sends contact force information to a robot hand in real time — no retargeting, no vision, just hands.

Dexterous manipulation with a multi-finger robot hand needs force — not just position. But most teleoperation setups either lose that information entirely or rely on vision that gets occluded the moment a finger actually touches something.


DexFIT is built around a glove that is kinematically identical to the target robot hand. Every joint maps 1-to-1, so no retargeting. Three fingertips are fitted with barometric tactile sensors that read contact pressure in real time. When the operator squeezes, the robot squeezes proportionally.


During deployment, a vision-free Diffusion Policy runs on joint positions and tactile signals only. An Adaptive Force Control loop watches task progress and stiffens the grasp when rotation stalls — handling the increasing friction that contact-rich tasks always throw at you near the end.

How it works

Force Transfer Pipeline

Two stages: force-informed data collection, then adaptive deployment.

Pipeline Diagram

논문 Fig. 5

assets/pipeline.png

① Force Generation — during data collection

Each time the operator's fingertip contacts an object, the max taxel reading drives additional joint flexion on the robot. The robot hand squeezes further than the glove angle alone would dictate — generating real, measurable contact force.

θ_cmd = θ_glove + K_j · (T_f / T_scale) K = 30 for DIP/PIP, K = 15 for MCP 3-finger avg RMSE = 0.63 N (Pearson r > 0.93)

② Adaptive Force Control — during deployment

At inference, the robot's own tactile sensors watch for stalls. When rotation slows below the target, the AFC loop ramps up flexion on the contacting fingers until the object starts moving again.

e_ω = clip(10 − ω, −10, 10) g_AFC = max(0, e_ω / 10 · G_max) G_max = 500

③ Vision-Free Diffusion Policy

A DDPM (1D U-Net + FiLM conditioning) trained on proprioceptive data only — joint positions, kinesthetic readings, binary tactile contact. No camera, no point cloud.

ObservationR³² (16 joint + 12 kinesthetic + 4 tactile)
Action16-dim joint position targets
Architecture1D U-Net, channels (64, 127, 256)
DiffusionT=100 train, DDIM-10 at inference
Training600 epochs, Adam 1e-4, cosine annealing

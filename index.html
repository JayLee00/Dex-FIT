Abstract
Acquiring high-quality demonstration data for dexterous robotic hand manipulation remains challenging because conventional methods either lose critical force information during collection or require complex, error-prone retargeting processes. We propose DexFIT (Dexterous Force-Informed Transfer), a system designed to transfer human contact forces to a robotic hand in real-time through a kinematically isomorphic wearable glove equipped with integrated fingertip tactile sensors. The glove's 16-DoF joint structure is mechanically identical to the target robot hand, thereby eliminating retargeting entirely. During data collection, a Force Generation (FG) module converts tactile readings from the operator's fingertips into supplementary joint angle commands. For autonomous deployment, an Adaptive Force Control (AFC) module monitors task progress via the robot's onboard tactile sensors and dynamically adjusts grasp forces. We train a vision-free Diffusion Policy on proprioceptive data and evaluate on a light bulb rotation task, achieving 100% task completion with a 34.5% reduction in task time and 16% reduction in energy consumption.

Method

DexFIT Pipeline

Two complementary force mechanisms bridging data collection and autonomous deployment.

📊

논문 Fig. 5: 전체 파이프라인 다이어그램

assets/pipeline.png
① Force Generation (FG) DATA COLLECTION

Operator wears DexFIT glove and physically manipulates the object. Fingertip tactile readings are converted to supplementary joint angle commands via:

θ_cmd = θ_glove + K_j · (T_f / T_scale)
Optimal gain K=30 selected via ATI Nano17 force reproduction experiment. 3-finger avg RMSE = 0.63 N, Pearson r > 0.93.

② Adaptive Force Control (AFC) DEPLOYMENT

At inference, the robot's onboard tactile sensors monitor contact. A velocity controller:

g_AFC = max(0, clip(10-ω,-10,10)/10 · G_max)
When rotation stalls, DIP joints receive additional flexion. Adapts to increasing socket friction beyond 300° rotation.

③ Vision-Free Diffusion Policy POLICY

DDPM trained on proprioceptive data only — no camera. Observation: 16 joint positions + 12 kinesthetic + 4 binary tactile = R32. Action: 16-dim joint position targets.

Architecture1D U-Net + FiLM
Diffusion stepsT=100 (DDIM-10)
Action chunkingT_obs=2, T_pred=16
Training600 epochs, Adam 1e-4

Comparison with Related Work
VISION-BASED
DexPilot / DexMV
Stereo/monocular hand pose estimation. Suffers from severe occlusion at contact points — exactly where force matters most. No tactile information.
ISOMORPHIC
DEXOP / DexUMI / KineDex
Eliminates retargeting. However, force is either implicit and uncontrolled (DEXOP), absent (DexUMI), or applied only at deployment (KineDex).
OURS
FORCE-INFORMED
DexFIT
Kinematically isomorphic and actively generates explicit, quantitative contact forces during data collection via wearable tactile sensors. First to close this gap.
